How Typing Accuracy is Measured

Accuracy is the percentage of words you typed correctly out of all words you attempted:

Accuracy = (Correct words ÷ Total words) × 100

Why accuracy matters more than raw speed

At 95% accuracy, 1 in 20 words is wrong. In a 60-WPM typist's output that's 3 errors per minute — errors that must be corrected, slowing effective output. True productivity comes from the combination of speed and accuracy, not speed alone.

Target accuracy benchmarks

  • Good: 95% — most professional situations
  • Very good: 97–98%
  • Excellent: 99%+ — data entry, medical transcription

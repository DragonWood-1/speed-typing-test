    About the 5 Minute Typing Test

    The 5 minute test is the gold standard for serious typists. Over five minutes, fatigue, focus, and consistency all come into play — making it a far truer measure of real-world performance than shorter tests.

    Why 5 minutes matters

    Your WPM on a 1 minute test can spike due to a short burst of fast typing. Over 5 minutes, your speed levels out to a reliable average that better represents how fast you actually work throughout the day.

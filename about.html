About SpeedType

Free typing tests built to help you measure and improve real typing skill.

Our Mission

SpeedType was created with one simple goal: to give anyone a fast, accurate, and completely free way to measure and improve their typing skills. Whether you are a student learning to type for the first time, a professional who spends all day at a keyboard, or a competitive typist chasing a new personal record, we want to give you the tools to track your progress and get better.

Typing is one of the most valuable and transferable skills in the modern world. Almost every job, every classroom, and every creative pursuit now involves a keyboard. Yet most people never receive formal typing instruction and simply muddle through with two or four fingers. A few minutes of focused practice each day can transform typing from a bottleneck into a genuine advantage — and that is exactly what SpeedType is designed to help you do.

What Makes SpeedType Different

Many typing tests only report a single raw speed number. We believe that speed without accuracy is meaningless, so every test we offer measures both — and shows you exactly where you went wrong.

  • Net and Gross WPM. We distinguish between the raw speed at which you type (gross WPM) and your effective speed after accounting for errors (net WPM), so you get an honest picture of your real performance.
  • Detailed accuracy tracking. After every test we show you a full list of the words you misspelled, comparing exactly what you typed against what was expected. This targeted feedback is the fastest way to fix recurring mistakes.
  • Real-time feedback. As you type, the current word highlights and turns red the instant your input diverges from the target, helping you build accurate muscle memory.
  • Content for everyone. We offer tailored tests for kids, students, teachers, and coders, along with tests themed around different languages and professions, so you can practice with material that is relevant to you.

How Our Tests Work

Each test presents you with a passage of real, meaningful text drawn from a large library of original writing on topics ranging from nature and science to history and daily life. The timer starts the moment you press your first key, so there is no pressure before you are ready. You type each word and press the spacebar to move on. When time runs out, you receive a complete breakdown of your words per minute, your accuracy percentage, and a combined score that rewards the balance of speed and precision.

Every passage is chained from a bank of distinct texts, which means you can take the test as many times as you like without memorizing the words — each attempt is a genuine measure of your skill.

Free and Accessible

SpeedType is and always will be free to use. There is no login, no download, and no software to install — everything runs directly in your web browser on any device, whether that is a desktop computer, a laptop, a tablet, or a phone. We support this free service through unobtrusive advertising, which allows us to keep building new features and content for our users.

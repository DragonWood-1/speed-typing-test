    Typing Tips for Kids

    Learning to type well is a superpower! Here are some tips to help you get faster:

    • Put your fingers on the home row: left fingers on A S D F, right fingers on J K L ;
    • Don't look at your keyboard — keep your eyes on the screen
    • It's okay to go slow at first. Accuracy comes before speed!
    • Practice a little every day and you will improve fast
    • Try to use all your fingers, not just your two pointer fingers

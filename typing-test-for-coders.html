    Typing Speed for Developers

    A developer who types 80 WPM instead of 40 WPM doesn't write twice as much code — but they do spend less cognitive energy on the physical act of typing, leaving more bandwidth for problem solving, design, and debugging.

    What to practice as a developer

    • Common programming terms: function, variable, iteration, recursion
    • Symbol fluency: brackets, underscores, colons, and pipes
    • Touch typing to reduce context switching between screen and keyboard

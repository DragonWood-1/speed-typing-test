    About the 1 Minute Typing Test

    The 1 minute typing test is the most popular benchmark for measuring typing speed. In just 60 seconds you get a clear snapshot of your words per minute (WPM) without the fatigue that comes from longer sessions.

    What is a good 1 minute WPM?

    • Beginner: 20–40 WPM
    • Average: 40–60 WPM
    • Proficient: 60–80 WPM
    • Advanced: 80–100 WPM
    • Expert: 100+ WPM

    Tips to improve in 60 seconds

    Focus on accuracy over speed — net WPM penalizes misspelled words. Keep your eyes on the passage, not your fingers, and use all ten fingers with proper home-row positioning.

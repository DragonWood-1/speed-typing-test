    About the 2 Minute Typing Test

    The 2 minute typing test gives a more stable WPM reading than the 1 minute test because small bursts of speed or slowdowns are averaged out over a longer session.

    Why take a 2 minute test?

    It's long enough to reveal your true sustained rhythm but short enough that focus stays sharp throughout. Many employers and typing certification programs use 2–3 minute tests as their standard benchmark.

How to Play Word Rush

  • A word appears — type it and press Space or Enter to submit
  • Type fast! Each word has a time limit shown by the shrinking bar
  • Miss a word and you lose one ♥ life — you have three
  • Build a combo by typing consecutive words correctly — it multiplies your score!
  • Game ends when all 50 words are done or you lose all lives

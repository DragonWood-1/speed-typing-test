    About the 3 Minute Typing Test

    The 3 minute typing test is the industry standard used by most employment agencies, government bodies, and schools when assessing typing proficiency. A WPM measured over 3 minutes reflects realistic daily typing speed far better than shorter tests.

    Professional benchmarks

    • Clerical/admin roles: 40+ WPM required
    • Data entry: 60+ WPM preferred
    • Court reporters: 225+ WPM (on stenotype)
    • General office work: 50–70 WPM typical

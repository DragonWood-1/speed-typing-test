Get In Touch

SpeedType is built and maintained by a small team that genuinely cares about helping people type faster and more accurately. Your feedback directly shapes what we build next, so please do not hesitate to reach out.

What You Can Contact Us About

  • Feedback and suggestions. Have an idea for a new test mode, a feature that would help you practice, or content you would like to see? Let us know.
  • Bug reports. If something is not working as expected — a timer glitch, a display issue on your device, or an incorrect result — tell us the details and we will investigate.
  • Content questions. Questions about how our scoring works, what counts as a word, or how accuracy is calculated are always welcome.
  • Business inquiries. For partnership, advertising, or other business matters, please reach out and include the details of your request.

Email

The best way to reach us is by email at hello@speedtype.example. We read every message and aim to respond within a few business days. When reporting a problem, it helps to include the device and browser you are using, along with a description of what you expected to happen and what actually happened.

Before You Write

Many common questions are answered directly on our homepage and in the information sections at the bottom of each test page. If you are curious about how words per minute is calculated, our WPM calculator explains the formulas in detail, and our accuracy calculator covers how error rates are measured.

Thank you for using SpeedType. Every message helps us make the site better for the thousands of people who use it to improve their typing every day.
